# UNSTAGED

UNSTAGED is a wedding planner for couples who care how their day looks and feels. This plugin brings your own wedding into Claude, so the planning you already have becomes something Claude can read and act on.

## What it does

The plugin pairs the UNSTAGED connector with four skills that know how a wedding is planned in UNSTAGED:

- **Quote to budget.** Paste the email or attach the PDF from a florist, caterer or photographer. Claude reads the amount and what it covers, and adds it to the right budget category as a quote you can compare with the others.
- **Guest list.** Hand over names in any shape (a note, a spreadsheet, a message from your mother) and they land on the guest list, grouped and on the right side, without duplicates. RSVPs and dietary notes are updated the same way.
- **Design brief.** Claude looks at the actual images in your moodboards, your palette and your Decor ideas, and writes a brief a florist or stylist can work from.
- **This week.** What to do now, what is due, and what is left to pay, from The Guide, your to-dos and your budget.

Everything Claude adds appears at once in the UNSTAGED iOS app and on the web.

## Use it

1. Install the plugin and open its Connectors tab.
2. Connect UNSTAGED and sign in with your UNSTAGED account.
3. On the consent page, choose area by area (planning, budget, vendors, guests, the day, design) whether Claude may read, read and edit, or not see it at all. You can change this at any time under Settings in UNSTAGED.
4. Ask in your own words: "Add this quote to our budget", "Here are the names from my side", "Write a brief for our florist", "What should we do this week?".

You need an UNSTAGED membership (seven-day free trial at [unstaged.se](https://unstaged.se) or in the iOS app) and a wedding set up in it.

## What Claude can and cannot do

Claude can read your planning and add to or update it. It can never delete anything, never change a payment that has been recorded, and never reach your membership or billing. Every change is recorded with its previous value so it can be reversed, and it shows up in the activity feed for your partner.

## Data

The plugin itself contains only text: these instructions and the address of the UNSTAGED connector. It runs no code and stores nothing.

When you use it, Claude sends requests to your UNSTAGED account at `unstaged.se` and receives the parts of your wedding you have allowed: budget, to-dos, vendors, guests, schedule, decisions, moodboard and Decor images, and creative direction. What you ask Claude to add (a budget row, guests, a to-do, an image from a link you give) is sent to the same place. Nothing is sent anywhere else. See the [privacy policy](https://unstaged.se/privacy).
